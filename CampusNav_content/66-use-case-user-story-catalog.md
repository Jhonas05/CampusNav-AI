# CampusNav AI — Use Case and User Story Catalog

## Guest/freshman/visitor
- As a user unfamiliar with campus, I can search a classroom/office/facility and see where it is.
- I can set my location through QR or manual selection and receive a valid route.
- I can switch 2D/3D without losing my destination/route.
- I can view public facility/service information.
- I can access approved emergency information.

## Student/parent
- I can view relevant official announcements/events/status information.
- I can find the office that handles a service even if I do not know its name.
- I can inspect approved room/class/personnel schedule information where permitted.

## Faculty/staff/personnel
- I can view relevant campus information and permitted schedule/assignment data.
- If the institution enables it, authorized users may check into permitted facilities.

## Facility/department admin
- I can maintain data that my role owns, such as facility information, hours, advisories, or assignments.

## Super admin
- I can administer users/roles, content, map/configuration, schedules, emergency records, and audit/governance functions subject to authorization.

## CLARA user
- I can ask a campus-domain question, and CLARA should call verified CampusNav tools/data rather than invent an answer.
