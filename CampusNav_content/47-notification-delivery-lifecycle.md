# CampusNav AI — Notification Delivery and Lifecycle Contract

## Centralization
The Dashboard is the main notification/status surface. Feature pages may show local status required to complete the current task.

## Categories
Source architecture supports concepts including:
- GENERAL
- ACADEMIC
- EVENT
- FACILITY
- SCHEDULE
- EMERGENCY
- SUSPENSION
- NAVIGATION

## Priority
- INFORMATIONAL
- NORMAL
- IMPORTANT
- URGENT

## Lifecycle
- draft
- scheduled
- published
- expired
- cancelled

## Audience
May target everyone or approved user groups such as students, parents, faculty, staff, school level, department/program/year/section, or another authorized group.

## Delivery channels
- in-app/dashboard is core
- web push is optional where implemented
- SMS is optional/future unless explicitly implemented and funded

## Truth rule
AI may rank/summarize an official record, but may not originate official emergency/suspension decisions.
