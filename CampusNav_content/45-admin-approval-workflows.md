# CampusNav AI — Admin Approval and Verification Workflows

## Principle
User/admin input does not automatically become verified institutional truth merely because it was submitted.

## User-reported map/facility issue
`Submitted → Pending Verification → Admin Review → Approved/Rejected → Official Update`

## Emergency data
`Draft/Imported → Authorized Safety/Facilities Review → Verified → Published/Active`
No AI-generated emergency route may bypass this flow.

## Schedule/personnel data
Should originate from an authorized academic/department source. Manual overrides/check-ins require appropriate role ownership and auditability.

## Announcements/suspensions
Only authorized school administration may publish official suspension/emergency decisions. AI may prioritize or explain but may not originate the decision.

## Map edits
Structural changes should create map/version change history and trigger route regression checks before becoming authoritative.

## Deletion
Prefer deactivation/archival when audit/history matters. Destructive deletion of authoritative records requires careful dependency checks.
