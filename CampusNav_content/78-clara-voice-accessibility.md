The CLARA tool, grounding, action and role rules in [17](17-clara-ai-contract.md) apply to every confirmed question. Default production builds omit the controls. ## A question from microphone to answer

1. Select **Speak to CLARA** to read the privacy notice and opt into spoken public replies. Choose English or Filipino. **Start microphone** initiates one capture. A secure context, an available recognizer and a browser-reported installed on-device language are prerequisites. A browser may conceal installed packs. The adapter never requests a language download.
2. Language discovery and microphone permission share a 20-second deadline starting on **Start microphone**. A late language result after timeout or cancellation cannot activate capture. Once listening, capture ends on **Finish speaking**, a final result, cancellation or the 15-second limit. The finalization timeout is four seconds. Denial, interruption, empty/oversized results and unavailable capabilities give a visible status with a typing fallback.
3. Review and edit the candidate (up to 500 characters), retry, discard or explicitly select **Send transcript**. Only confirmed text reaches the existing `ClaraContext.send` and `askClara`. The candidate lives in component memory. The sent text enters the same in-memory conversation as typed questions. Navigation still requires selecting the existing destination action.
4. CLARA displays its ordinary grounded reply. The verified public facility/location, public status and configured-service answer builders can label a reply `speechScope: "PUBLIC"`. Playback also requires assistant authorship, `VERIFIED_DIRECT` mode and at least one source. Unclassified, personal, restricted, ambiguous, FAQ and failure responses stay in text. Adding a new eligible answer type requires review of the entire rendered answer text.
5. An in-memory request marker ties automatic speech to the reviewed voice turn. An active, visible, online and unmuted panel passes the exact visible answer body to a local device voice. Typed turns offer **Read Aloud** for eligible replies. Pause, resume, stop and mute expose playback state. The browser start event sets the speaking indicator. Missing local voices, playback errors and a five-second start timeout leave the text available.
6. New turns supersede earlier audio. Exit, close/minimize, route or account changes, hidden page, offline transition and unmount dispose of the session. CLARA follows its existing offline policy. The camera guide has separate video-only ownership.

`src/services/claraSpeech.js` provides recognition with start/stop/cancel and status/transcript callbacks, and synthesis with play/pause/resume/stop. Browser interfaces are injected for deterministic tests. `ClaraVoice` owns the draft and opt-in state. Neither media, transcript drafts nor voice identifiers enter browser storage, telemetry or the service worker. Browser-supplied speech support varies. [Web Speech on-device availability](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SpeechRecognition/available_static)

| Question | Finding and release gate |

| Availability and cost | Cloudflare lists `@cf/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo` at USD 0.000513 per audio minute. Workers AI publishes a shared daily allocation of 10,000 Neurons and paid-plan overages. The school's entitlement and available balance are unknown. A future service needs atomic application-level ceilings, throttling, timeouts and fail-closed exhaustion. Procurement and the school DPO must review applicable service/model terms, contractual processing location, exceptions, consent and under-18 use before any pilot. Current evidence establishes no approved audience or region. [Data usage](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/) · [Developer Platform terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/service-specific-terms-developer-platform/) |
| Formats and accuracy | Cloudflare demonstrates base64-encoded MP3 chunks. Its model exposes an optional language and a transcription task. Browser codec/container acceptance, application byte/duration caps and English, Filipino and mixed Taglish quality need an approved non-personal test corpus. No language quality threshold has been accepted. [Cloudflare audio input example](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/guides/tutorials/build-a-workers-ai-whisper-with-chunking/) |
| Server boundary | Hosted STT is **BLOCKED**. Before implementation, approve MIME/content validation, server-side byte/duration enforcement, per-user and aggregate budgets, transient processing and log suppression. Credentials belong on a trusted server. The app has no speech endpoint, binding, paid fallback or audio upload. Text generation has separate gates under [76](76-clara-rag-provider-readiness.md). |
